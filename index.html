The Guesthouse

A small house on the quiet side of Buea.

Mount Fable holds three rooms under one roof — no lobby music, no queue at reception, no corridor of identical doors. You arrive, the gate closes behind you, and the garden is all there is.

We furnished it the way we would furnish a home: timber, stone, deep chairs, good linen and as much daylight as the windows will take. Breakfast is made in the morning kitchen and served wherever you happen to be sitting.

Mount Fable Guesthouse is a fictional property created to demonstrate this website format. The story, rooms and photographs are illustrative.

Location

Buea, on the green slope of the mountain.

Buea sits on the flank of Mount Cameroon, where the air cools a few degrees on the way up and the rain arrives most afternoons and leaves again.

From the house it is a short drive down to the town centre, the market and the university, and a longer one to the black sand at Mile 4 and the road that climbs toward the mountain.
